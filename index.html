About

Rules are just suggestions
until they're fun.

Every project starts the same way — a single mechanic, tested until it either breaks or becomes the whole game. I like systems you can hold in your head, worlds small enough to finish and strange enough to remember. Ultraviolet. is that habit with a name on it.

Journey

How the studio got here.

  1. 2019

    First jam, first bug

    Made Driftwood in a weekend and learned that "just ship it" is a design philosophy.

  2. 2021

    Studio gets a name

    Ultraviolet. becomes the banner for everything after Static Pilgrim's surprise release.

  3. 2022

    First narrative game

    Glass Orchard — the first project built around a feeling instead of a mechanic.

  4. 2023

    Neonfall ships

    The biggest project yet, and the first one with a wishlist page before a prototype.

  5. Now

    Next thing, unannounced

    Currently prototyping. Ask nicely and I might show you a build.
